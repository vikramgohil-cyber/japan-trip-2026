Day 1 • Sun, 20 Sep 2026 Melbourne → Transit

Melbourne Tullamarine Departure 🛫

Assemble at MEL Terminal 2 at 8:30 PM for baggage drop. Depart at 11:35 PM aboard Singapore Airlines flight SQ218 flying overnight to Singapore.

Day 2 • Mon, 21 Sep 2026 Tokyo Bay Arrival

Tokyo Arrival, Shibuya Crossing & Takeshita Street 🗼

Land at Narita T1 at 5:30 PM (SQ012). Private transfer to ANA Holiday Inn Tokyo Bay. Head into Shibuya to witness the scramble crossing, Hachiko statue, Shibuya Sky sunset views, and sweet crepes along Harajuku Takeshita Street.

Veg Spot: Ain Soph. Ripple (vegan burgers & fries) or Nagi Shokudo in Shibuya.
Day 3 • Tue, 22 Sep 2026 Historic & Futuristic

Senso-ji Asakusa, Nakamise & Tokyo Skytree ⛩️

Immerse in historic Asakusa, walk the lantern gate at Senso-ji temple, sample traditional treats along Nakamise Arcade, and shoot 450m into the sky at Tokyo Skytree.

Veg Spot: T’s Restaurant in Ueno (famous rich sesame vegan ramen) or Saido in Jiyugaoka.
Day 4 • Wed, 23 Sep 2026 • Confirmed Tour Mt. Fuji & Onsen Soak

Mount Fuji, Lake Kawaguchiko & Onsen Hot Spring 🗻♨️

Guided tour of Mt. Fuji 5th station, Chureito Pagoda views, and Lake Kawaguchiko. Top off the day relaxing in an authentic Japanese open-air onsen bath with views of the peak.

Day 5 • Thu, 24 Sep 2026 • Confirmed Booking Toyosu Digital Art

teamLab Planets TOKYO & Akihabara Electric Town 🎨⚡

Confirmed entry for the barefoot sensory experience at teamLab Planets in Toyosu. Afternoon gaming, anime culture, and retro collectibles in Akihabara.

Veg Spot: Komaki Shokudo (Akihabara Buddhist temple Shojin Ryori) or Ain Soph. Journey Ginza.
Day 6 • Fri, 25 Sep 2026 Luggage Forwarding Eve

Ginza Flagship Uniqlo & Art Aquarium Museum 🛍️🐠

Browse the 12-floor Ginza Uniqlo flagship and the illuminated Art Aquarium Museum Ginza. Hand over main luggage to the hotel concierge tonight for porter delivery directly to Kyoto.

Day 7 • Sat, 26 Sep 2026 Bullet Train

Shinkansen to Kyoto & Nijo Castle 🏯

Board the Tokaido Shinkansen to Kyoto. Check in to KAYA Kyoto Nijo Castle BW Signature Collection. Evening walk around the lantern-lit streets of Gion.

Day 8 • Sun, 27 Sep 2026 Eastern Kyoto

Fushimi Inari Torii Gates & Hokan-ji Pagoda ⛩️

Morning trek through the thousands of vermilion gates at Fushimi Inari. Explore Higashiyama and photograph the historic Yasaka Pagoda (Hokan-ji).

Veg Spot: VOG Kyoto (offers Jain-friendly and strictly vegetarian culinary creations).
Day 9 • Mon, 28 Sep 2026 Wellness & Relaxation

Kyoto Spa Day & Arashiyama Bamboo Grove 🎋💆

Relaxing morning at Kyoto day spas and hot springs. Optional afternoon visit to Arashiyama Bamboo Grove and the Kamo River.

Day 10 • Tue, 29 Sep 2026 • Day Trip Sacred Deer Park

Nara Day Excursion: Mochi Pounding, Chopsticks & Deer 🦌🔨

Watch the thunderous rapid mochi-pounding show at Nakatanidou, take part in an artisan chopstick craft workshop, feed the bowing deer in Nara Park, and visit the Great Buddha at Todai-ji.

Veg Spot: Kinatei (Japanese veg sets) or Nara Mahoroba Daibutsu Ramen.
Day 11 • Wed, 30 Sep 2026 Transfer to Osaka

Nishiki Market & Private Transfer to Osaka 🍱🚐

Sample savory skewers at Nishiki Market in Kyoto. Board our pre-booked private transfer to RIHGA Royal Hotel Osaka.

Day 12 • Thu, 01 Oct 2026 Osaka Neon

Osaka Castle, Shinsaibashi & Dotonbori Neon 🏰✨

Climb Osaka Castle tower, stroll Shinsaibashi shopping arcade, take the Tombori River cruise, and snap a selfie in front of the illuminated Glico Running Man sign.

Veg Spot: Paprika Shokudo Vegan or Green Earth Osaka.
Day 13 • Fri, 02 Oct 2026 • Confirmed Entry Super Nintendo World

Universal Studios Japan (USJ) 🍄⚡

Full day in Super Nintendo World, Mario Kart Koopa’s Challenge, Yoshi's Adventure, and the Wizarding World of Harry Potter.

Day 14 • Sat, 03 Oct 2026 Osaka Bay & Departure

Kaiyukan Aquarium, Giant Ferris Wheel & Narita Flight 🦈🎡

Visit Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan and ride the Tempozan Ferris wheel. Shinkansen/express transfer to Narita Airport T1 for Singapore Airlines flight SQ011 at 7:00 PM.

Day 15 • Sun, 04 Oct 2026 Landing Home

Singapore Connection & Melbourne Arrival 🛬

Transit in Singapore Changi. Connect onto SQ207 departing 7:50 AM, landing safely back home in Melbourne at 6:10 PM.

Japan Packing Checklist

0 of 8 packed

Cash & Seven Bank ATMs

Many temple stalls, street food vendors, and ramen machines only take cash. 7-Eleven (Seven Bank) ATMs have full English menus and charge zero ATM fee for foreign cards.

Luggage Delivery (Takkyubin)

Don't haul large suitcases onto the bullet train. Yamato Transport counters in hotel concierges will ship luggage between Tokyo and Kyoto overnight for ~¥2,500/bag.
